FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I submit my own recipes to FlavorBook?

Yes, absolutely! We love community creations. You can submit your recipe drafts, detailed instructions, and high-quality photographs to submissions@flavorbook.com. Our chef crew reviews all entries and publishes selected recipes monthly.

Are these recipe guides completely offline-friendly?

Once you load the catalog recipes, they are cached in your local environment. Your saved favorites list is fully persistent using Local Storage, allowing you to access ingredient checklists even without active internet connections.

Do you provide gluten-free or allergen-specific filter sets?

We are currently updating our database schema to tag gluten-free, vegan, and dairy-free options. The updated category badges will be rolled out in early winter, offering extensive dietary restrictions sorting.
